# PwarMyar Advertisement Guide

## Short Description

PwarMyar is a simple local POS software made for small and medium shops in Myanmar. It helps shop owners sell faster, manage products, track stock, record purchases and expenses, check customer debts, and print receipts from one easy desktop app.

PwarMyar is designed for daily shop use: simple, clear, fast, and not complicated.

## Target Audience

PwarMyar is suitable for:

- Mini marts
- Grocery shops
- Phone and accessories shops
- Cosmetics shops
- Clothing shops
- Stationery shops
- Wholesale and retail shops
- Small warehouses
- Family-owned shops
- Any small to medium business that wants a simple POS system

## Main Advantages

### 1. Easy To Use

PwarMyar is built for normal shop users, not only accountants or IT people. The screens are simple and clear, so staff can learn quickly.

### 2. Works Locally On One Computer

Shop data is stored on the user’s own computer. The app can run without depending on internet or cloud servers.

### 3. Fast Selling Screen

The Sell screen is designed for quick checkout. Users can search products, scan barcode, choose price type, add items to cart, receive payment, and print receipt.

### 4. Myanmar Shop Friendly

PwarMyar supports Myanmar language, English language, and Myanmar currency display with `Ks`.

### 5. Stock Control

When products are sold, stock decreases automatically. When purchases are recorded, stock increases automatically. Low-stock products are easy to check.

### 6. Customer Debt Tracking

Users can record customer credit sales, customer payments, and shop payouts. This helps shop owners know who still owes money.

### 7. Purchases And Suppliers

Shop owners can record product purchases from suppliers and track supplier information.

### 8. Expenses Tracking

Daily shop expenses can be recorded and checked in reports.

### 9. Reports For Owners

Owners can check sales, payments, profit estimate, expenses, top products, debt, and stock information.

### 10. Receipt Printing

PwarMyar supports receipt printing and receipt preview. Shop details such as shop name, address, phone number, and footer text can be configured.

### 11. Portable Desktop App

Users can open PwarMyar like a normal Windows desktop app. No complicated setup is needed for normal use.

### 12. Data Backup

Users can back up shop data and restore it when needed.

## How To Use PwarMyar

### Step 1: Add Products

Open the Products screen and add product name, category, barcode, cost, stock, and selling prices.

### Step 2: Add Stock With Purchases

Open Purchases and record products bought from suppliers. Product stock will increase automatically.

### Step 3: Sell Products

Open Sell, search or scan barcode, add products to cart, enter paid amount, choose payment method, and click Checkout.

### Step 4: Print Receipt

After checkout, print the receipt for the customer.

### Step 5: Track Customers

If a customer does not pay full amount, select the customer. The unpaid amount will be added to customer debt.

### Step 6: Record Expenses

Open Expenses and record daily shop expenses like rent, transport, staff cost, or other shop costs.

### Step 7: Check Reports

Open Reports to see sales, payment methods, expenses, profit estimate, top products, and customer debt.

### Step 8: Backup Data

Use Settings > Data to save a backup regularly.
